Open **[changerelay.vercel.app](https://changerelay.vercel.app)** in a desktop browser. Allow about four minutes. Phone Events/Preview layouts were inspected, but the complete phone journey is not verified.

**What to say:** “When an event moves, ChangeRelay shows who and what is affected, lets the organiser review the exact plan, and keeps a record of each update and any recovery.”

Current verification: **202 automated tests passed**, plus **27 frontend contract groups**. The real event remains unchanged by the preview.

## 1. Show the safe sample

1. Choose **Try sample workspace**.
2. Open **AI Workshop** and choose **Plan a change**. Its starting schedule is 31 October, 2–3 pm IST.
3. Propose **4 pm** and open the preview. Point out Riya's overlapping welcome-desk assignment and the Calendar/Sheets/people impact cards.
4. Choose **Edit proposal** and change the start to **5 pm**. Explain that the original one-hour duration is preserved and the recorded overlap clears.

Sample participants are fictional. No Google account changes or real emails occur in this workspace.

## 2. Show a failure and recovery

1. In the sample form, turn on **Demonstrate a sample spreadsheet failure and recovery**.
2. Preview, then choose **Approve sample change**.
3. Open **Activity**. Show Calendar verified while the sample spreadsheet needs attention.
4. Choose **Retry failed step only**. Show the recovered receipt and explain that the already verified Calendar work was preserved.

The hosted conflict and controlled failure have been observed. The current recovery attempt is awaiting confirmation; if it remains incomplete, show the honest saved status instead of calling it recovered.

The hosted sample is temporary and can reset. Use the signed-in live workspace to demonstrate saved account data; do not promise that a sample survives a server change.

## 3. Show the real live preview

1. Open **[Sign in](https://changerelay.vercel.app/signin)** if needed. Use the same organiser account already connected to Google; keep passwords private.
2. Show the three connected apps and the saved demo event mapping. Open event details to show the saved schedule and read-only roster.
3. In **Plan a change**, keep announcements **off**. Enter **“Move this workshop to 31 October 2026 at 5 pm.”** Use **Fill draft with AI**, then review the fields yourself.
4. Create a preview. Show the original 2–3 pm schedule and proposed 5–6 pm schedule, known conflicts, unavailable checks and exact change summary.
5. **Stop at the preview. Do not press the live approval button.** The owner declined applying this real reschedule; the real event remains 2–3 pm IST.

Hosted owner sign-in, the three existing connections, saved details and saved review have been checked. The final production refresh is in progress; use the existing demo event and displayed IST timezone until the release check finishes.

## Useful points to explain

- AI suggests a draft; the organiser decides. It cannot select recipients or approve a change.
- Calendar and Sheets are checked before any approved announcement is handled.
- Recorded volunteer conflicts are warnings; participant/private-calendar availability is not checked.
- Recovery preserves verified work and keeps uncertain outcomes visible.
- Preferences change display timezone and the announcement default. Help and saved setup labels make onboarding easier; they do not edit the roster.
- Privacy controls can pause/resume an integration or clear saved event data with guarded confirmation. Do not click them during judging: they were covered by tests, while the owner's actual controls have not been exercised. Clearing keeps the account and connections; it is not account deletion.

**Never send to fictional `example.com` addresses.** Live sending to them is blocked. A real update or email needs a separately reviewed and approved plan with authorised recipients. Earlier testing included one approved self-test email; the complete real reschedule-and-announcement journey was not performed.
